### Added — surface diffusion in the macropore term
- The generic mass-transfer coefficient (`series_resistance`) has a new switch `surface` under `macropore`: surface diffusion of the adsorbed phase in parallel with the pore diffusion. The macropore term becomes K τ R² / (c (ε_p D + τ K D_s)), i.e. k_macro = (c/R²)(ε_p D/(τ K) + D_s), with c = 15 (sphere), 8 (cylinder) or 3 (slab) and K the capacity factor of the model's mode. D_s = D_s0 exp(−E_s/(R T)) is one effective surface diffusivity, with the new leaves `surface_diffusivity` and `surface_activation_energy` (and `slow_` for a dual-rate slow site). The display equation and the deck view show the parallel form.
- A deck warning when `surface` is on with the capacity-factor mode `none` (the surface term needs K), and a deck error for a negative `surface_diffusivity`.
- `MassTransferResistances` and `MassTransferCoefficientSeriesResistance` are at cereal version 3. Decks from earlier versions load with `surface` off, so their results do not change; with `surface` on and D_s0 = 0 the coefficient is the same double as with it off. Re-saved decks gain the `Surface` switch and the two leaves.
