### Added — named sources and sinks
- A cycle can hold named **sources** (inlets attach to them) and **sinks** (outlets attach to them): `adsim::Reservoir` in `adsim/boundary_conditions/reservoir.h`, kept in `Cycle::reservoirs_`. A reservoir holds a gas state (temperature, pressure, mole fractions). An endpoint (a flow stream or a pressure boundary) that is attached to one takes its temperature, its mole fractions and, where it has a pressure target (a pressure boundary, the back pressure of a valve), its pressure. The flow, the valve, the pump and the ramp shape stay on the endpoint, so two feed steps can share one source at different flows.
- Parameter paths `reservoir[ID].pressure` and, for a source, `.temperature` and `.y[Component]` (alias `reservoir[Name]...`). A sink has a pressure only, because an outlet is zero-gradient in temperature and composition. The registry lists these values on the reservoir and not on the attached endpoints.
- Edit cascades in `adsim/objects/deck_edit.h`: `AddReservoir`, `AddReservoirFromEndpoint` (make a source or sink from an endpoint's present values and attach it), `RemoveReservoir`, `RenameReservoir`, `AttachReservoir`, `DetachMismatchedReservoirs`. Builder: `build::AddReservoir`, `build::AttachReservoir`, `build::ReservoirKinds`. A copied step stays attached, a component edit keeps the reservoir mole fractions in step, and a boundary or stream kind switch keeps the attachment.
- Deck view: the list `reservoirs` (id, name, kind, attached endpoints, values) and `reservoir` on each stream and pressure end.
- Python: `sim.add_source`, `sim.add_sink`, `sim.reservoirs`, `endpoint.attach`, `endpoint.detach`, `endpoint.reservoir`, and `sim.edit.add_reservoir`, `reservoir_from_endpoint`, `remove_reservoir`, `rename_reservoir`, `attach_reservoir`. `to_script` writes them.
- The deck checks refuse an inlet on a sink, an outlet on a source and two reservoirs with one name.
- Deck format: `Cycle` version 3 (`Reservoirs`), `IBoundaryStream` and `PressureBoundaryCondition` version 6 (`Reservoir_ID`). Earlier decks load without reservoirs. See docs/sources-and-sinks.md.
