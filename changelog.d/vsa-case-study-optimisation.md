### Added — re-optimising the CO₂ VSA case study under plant constraints
- `examples/perez_2019_case_study_optimise.py`: NSGA-II (pymoo) over the device settings and schedule of `Perez2019_CaseStudy_Base` and `_Base_LPP` (feed time, blower flow, vacuum-pump power, blowdown share, valve coefficients, the LPP feed share), each candidate run to cyclic steady state in a process pool and warm-started from the nearest cyclic steady state its worker has (`skarstrom.optimisation.run_warm`). Two objective sets: specific electrical energy (kWh per tonne CO₂) against productivity, subject to the purity and recovery targets and the plant limits; and purity against recovery, with or without the plant limits, for the comparison with the paper. Constraints, as NSGA-II constraint violations normalised by their bounds: purity and recovery targets, approach to fluidisation ≤ 1 and bed pressure drop per metre (operating-limit report and the pressure profile, every step), the lowest blowdown and evacuation pressure ≥ `variable[p_evac_floor]`, cyclic steady state reached with a healthy run, and optionally no backflow through an unconnected outlet. Every evaluation is appended to `results.csv` (variables, objectives, each constraint's value and margin, CSS cycle, cycles run, warm start, wall time); running the same command again resumes the search from the file.
- "Re-optimising under constraints" in the case-study documentation: how to run it, what each constraint does, and the constrained, unconstrained and paper fronts.
