### Changed — reported KPIs on the exact integrals
- The reported KPIs (purity, recovery, productivity, specific electrical energy, production rate, `mass_balance_error`, and the moles fed behind the reaction KPIs) now use the KPI quadratures the solver integrates alongside the solution, not the trapezoid over the logged samples. Reported KPIs move in about the fourth significant figure. The trapezoid values are the new `trapezoid_*` fields of `KPIRecord` (Python `sim.kpis()`, `kpi_table()`, CLI `--json`, the KPI manifest). They replace the `quadrature_*` fields and `quadrature_mass_balance_error` (renamed `trapezoid_mass_balance_error`) added in this release's KPI quadratures entry, which never shipped. An endpoint without quadratures (turned off, or a failed step) falls back to the trapezoid. `KPIParams::integration_` now defaults to `KPIIntegration::QUADRATURE`.
- At an outlet, an endpoint's component flow, logged and integrated, is its molar flow times the bed-end face mole fraction the species balance convects, not the bed-end average (#171). The cycle component balance now closes to about 1e-7 on Perez 2019 A–C (about 1e-5 before). The logged stream composition keeps the bed-end sampling, so connected streams are unchanged. A buffer tank's average composition is built from the component flows, so it moves by the same small amount.
- `productivity_volume` and the bed-volume space-time yield divide by the packed cells' volume, without the two ghost cells (and without void end cells) (#201). `productivity_volume` rises by (N+2)/N for N cells.
