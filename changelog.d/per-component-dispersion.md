### Added — axial dispersion with a coefficient per component
- The Ruthven and Taylor–Aris dispersion models take a `component_diffusivity` choice (registry path `layer[ID].component_diffusivity`). `shared` (the default) keeps one molecular diffusivity for every component. `mixture_averaged` uses each component's own mixture-averaged diffusivity D_m,i (Chapman–Enskog binary diffusivities with Blanc's law, as the macropore resistance uses), so each component has its own D_ax,i.
- With different coefficients, the component balance takes the self-consistent dispersive flux of Ramshaw (1990), written for the molar-average velocity: J_i = −ε_b C (D_ax,i ∂y_i/∂z − y_i Σ_j D_ax,j ∂y_j/∂z). The fluxes sum to zero through every face to round-off, so the total moles are conserved and the overall balance keeps no dispersion term. The logged axial dispersion coefficient is then Σ_i y_i D_ax,i.
- `DispersionModelRuthven` and `DispersionModelTaylorAris` are at cereal version 2. Decks saved before load as `shared`, and their results are unchanged bit for bit.
