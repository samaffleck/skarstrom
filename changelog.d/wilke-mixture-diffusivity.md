### Fixed — the mixture diffusivity rule is named after Wilke
- The docs called the effective molecular diffusivity of a component in a mixture, D_i,m = (1 − y_i) / Σ_j≠i (y_j / D_ij), "Blanc's law". Its usual name is Wilke's equation (Wilke 1950); Blanc's law (1908) is its trace limit. The docs, code comments and paper READMEs now say Wilke's equation, and the internal function `BlanksLaw` is renamed `WilkeMixtureDiffusivity`. The values do not change.
